Our Leader

CA ANURAG BANSAL

Anurag Bansal, Founder of Bansal & Agrawal, is a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) since 1998. With over two and a half decades of professional experience, he has been instrumental in advising a diverse clientele on securing government subsidies, financial assistance, and concessions under various Central and State Government schemes.

Throughout his career, Mr. Bansal has led teams in executing a wide range of high-impact assignments, including Statutory Audits, Concurrent Audits, Internal Audits, and Due Diligence exercises for Government Departments and private sector entities alike.

His expertise extends beyond domestic boundaries—he has successfully supported clients in making cross-border investments and setting up industrial ventures internationally, specifically in East African countries and Middle East regions. Backed by more than 25 years of experience in Tax Advisory and Assurance Services, Mr. Bansal brings a rare blend of strategic insight, technical proficiency, and practical business acumen to the firm's leadership.
